About

Shangshi Liu 刘上石

I am an Associate Research Scientist at the Yale School of the Environment and the Yale Center for Natural Carbon Capture. Before this I was a postdoctoral researcher at Yale, and previously at the University of Manchester. I hold a PhD in ecology from the Institute of Botany, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I am a soil biogeochemist and ecosystem ecologist interested in how global environmental change has eroded soil carbon sequestration and other ecosystem functions, and in why some ecosystems keep functioning under stress while others are more vulnerable.

Building on these mechanistic insights, I work on practical ways to restore and manage terrestrial ecosystems. I am particularly interested in combining complementary management interventions to maximise ecosystem service multifunctionality and promote equity among ecosystem services, across grasslands and croplands.

At Yale, I synthesise and generate evidence on whether regenerative agricultural practices enhance soil carbon and protect soil health, to give a more transparent, evidence-based view of their climate mitigation and adaptation benefits.

I also serve as an Associate Editor at the Journal of Ecology, the flagship outlet of the British Ecological Society and a fully double-blind journal that aims to make peer review more equitable.
