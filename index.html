About me

I am a soil biogeochemist and ecosystem ecologist working on soil carbon cycling, plant–soil interactions and ecosystem restoration. I am an Associate Research Scientist at the Yale School of the Environment and an Associate Editor at the Journal of Ecology.

I received my PhD in ecology from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and trained as a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Manchester (UK) and then at Yale, where I held a Yale Center for Natural Carbon Capture Postdoctoral Fellowship.
